About us

Made one stem at a time

A small studio making flowers by hand — the kind that outlast the occasion they were bought for.

What we make

Meemi Bloom makes flower bouquets and home decor pieces entirely by hand, from soft chenille stems wired and shaped petal by petal. They are not fresh flowers, not dried ones, and not moulded plastic. They are made the way a knitted thing is made — slowly, by someone sitting with it.

A single tulip takes several minutes. A lily with its curled stamens takes longer. A full bouquet is a few evenings' work, which is why we ask for a little notice and why no two are ever quite identical.

Why handmade

Fresh flowers are lovely for four days. Then someone has to throw them away, usually on the day the occasion stops feeling special.

These do the opposite. They sit on a shelf a year later and still look like the day they arrived. There is no water to change, nothing drops on the table, and there is no pollen at all — which matters more than people expect in a house with allergies or a small child.

The other thing handmade gives you is colour. We are not limited by what the market has that week. If you want teal and white, or rust and cream, or a shade matched to a saree, we can simply make it.

How we work

Most orders start as a WhatsApp message, and that is deliberate. A short conversation tells us far more than a dropdown menu — who it is for, what the occasion is, what colours live in the room it will sit in.

We agree the design and the price before anything begins. When it is finished we photograph it and send it to you. If it is not what you pictured, we change it before it goes anywhere.

What we care about

  • Made properly. Wired by hand, stem by stem, not assembled from kits.
  • Any colour, any season. Nothing depends on what is in the market that week.
  • Honest pricing. We tell you what a budget can and cannot buy before you commit.
  • Built to keep. A gift that is still there next year.

We deliver across Bengaluru and the areas around it.
