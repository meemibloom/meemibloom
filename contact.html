What to include in your first message

You do not need all of this, but the more you send, the faster we can price it accurately:

  • The occasion, and who it is for
  • The date you need it by
  • The colours you want — a photo or a fabric swatch is ideal
  • Roughly what you want to spend
  • The delivery address

If you have seen something of ours you liked, send that photo too. We can make it again in your colours.
