What we make

Three things, done properly

Handmade bouquets

Tulips, lilies, daisies and blossoms, each petal shaped from soft chenille stems and wired together by hand. Wrapped, ribboned and finished like any florist's bouquet — it just doesn't wilt.

Gifts and occasions

Birthdays, anniversaries, weddings, farewells and thank-yous. Because nothing fades, these stay on a shelf long after the day itself — which is rather the point of a gift.

Home decor

Permanent arrangements for a table, a shelf or a corner that never gets enough light. No water, no dropped petals, no pollen — useful if anyone in the house has allergies.

How ordering works

Three steps, one conversation

There is no cart to fill. You tell us the occasion and the colours, we agree a price, and it is made after you approve it.

  1. Tell us

    Message us with the occasion, the date you need it, the colours you have in mind and roughly what you want to spend.

  2. We make it

    Each piece is wired stem by stem, so a bouquet takes a few days. We send you a photo of the finished piece before it leaves us.

  3. Delivered to you

    We deliver across Bengaluru and the areas around it. Nothing perishes on the way, so there is no rush and no cold chain to worry about.

Have a colour in mind?

Send us the shade, the occasion and your budget. We will come back with what we can make.
